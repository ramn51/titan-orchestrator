How to read Live graphs · signals and bottlenecks

Queue composition · Stacked areas show total jobs and which lane occupies the most. Ready rising while running stays flat means dispatch or capacity is blocked. Blocked means unmet DAG parents.

Dispatch loop duration · Spikes mean scheduler placement itself is slow. Compare with phase costs below to locate the step.

Throughput · Completions should rise as load arrives. Failure spikes alongside throughput indicate retries or bad tasks.

Failure rate · A rising percentage means a larger share of settled jobs failed; inspect recent spans and worker logs.

Saturation · Near 100% with a growing ready queue means more worker slots or less work per task are needed.

Where dispatch time goes · The phase table shows min, median, max, and share. A dominant store phase points to persistence; send points to worker RPC.

Store latency · Rising read/write time with dispatch store time suggests the store is slowing admission or completion.

Heartbeat round-trip · Sustained or one-worker spikes suggest a slow or unreachable worker. Select a fleet node for its own history.

Worker slot utilization · One worker pinned while others are idle suggests placement imbalance or capability constraints.

Oldest waiting job · Age rising while throughput is low reveals starvation; check the queue board for its waiting reason.

Queue wait tail · p95 far above p50 means a subset waits much longer. Inspect priority, capability, and worker placement.

Backlog versus drain · If ready jobs grow faster than completions, the cluster is falling behind.

Capability debt · Parked jobs need a capability no registered worker offers; add that worker or correct the job requirement.

Placement imbalance · A persistent high spread means work is concentrated on some workers despite spare slots elsewhere.

Retry pressure · Rising retries can hide unstable workers or scripts; compare with failure rate and recent events.

Fleet scale / utilization · Worker and available-slot steps show scale-out or scale-in. Used slots close to available slots means the fleet is saturated. A flat worker count during sustained saturation points to an autoscaler limit or failure.

How to read Analytics graphs · run comparisons

Success rate · Rolling share of completed terminal attempts. A dip means more failures or cancellations; retries are separate attempts.

Job duration distribution · Bars show how many jobs fall in each runtime bucket. A growing long tail points to slow tasks.

Queue wait percentiles · p50 is typical; p95 and p99 expose delayed outliers. High waits with short job durations indicate scheduling pressure.

Per-worker slot utilization · Compare workers over the same time window; a repeatedly full worker beside idle peers suggests skew.

Does priority work? · Values are numeric priorities; higher numbers should usually wait less when jobs compete. The counts below show sample size.

Pipeline duration trend · Each color is one DAG. Points are runs; a downward line is faster. The table counts runs and compares the latest with the previous run. A change under 5% is stable.
