Execution Timeline

Every recorded job on an absolute time axis. Bars are real start and end times, so overlap is real parallelism — not layout.

How to read this — click to collapse
grey prefix
Time the job sat queued after becoming eligible. A long grey bar means the scheduler, not your script, is what made it slow.
amber prefix
Queued more than 2s — starved. One job stuck at the head of the priority queue starves everything behind it, and this is how you see it.
coloured bar
Actual execution. Length is real duration and horizontal position is real start time, so two bars that overlap genuinely ran at the same moment.
amber outline
On the critical path. Speeding up anything without this outline will not make the run finish sooner.
#2, #3
Retry attempt. Each attempt is its own span, so a job that failed twice before succeeding shows three bars.
PERM / EPH
Whether the worker that ran it is a permanent node or an ephemeral one the scaler spawned and may reclaim.
hover a row
Queue wait, execution time, worker, failure reason, and parents.

Critical path is the longest chain of dependent jobs by wall time — shortening anything off it will not make the run finish sooner. Parallelism is total job time divided by wall clock: 1.0 means everything ran one after another, higher means real overlap.
